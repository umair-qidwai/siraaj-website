Siraaj Institute

Our Mission

Education, Enlightenment, Engagement

Through a part-time seminary at NoorOhio, Siraaj aims to provides Islamic education with a unified approach. Siraaj will provide intensive programs, curriculum development & teacher training to promote sacred knowledge in Columbus.

Program of Study

Fall & Spring

Graduate from the Part-Time Seminary by completing 8 core credits, 8 elective credits, and 4 Arabic credits. The seminary may be completed in a minimum of two years across four semesters. Students will also have the option to go at their own pace and complete it over a longer period.

Arabic

Comprehensive study of Arabic language, grammar, and classical texts.

Fiqh

In-depth exploration of Islamic jurisprudence, laws, and ethical principles.

Hadith

Learn about the sayings and traditions of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

Tafsir

Explore the interpretations and meanings of the Quranic verses.

History

Examination of Seerah, Islamic history, significant events, and influential figures.

Tazkiyah

Learning spiritual purification and self-improvement in Islam.

Siraaj Summer Semester

June 23rd - August 13th

Beyond Culture: Anchoring Your Islam in an Age of Influencers

Explore how Islam and the Sunnah were preserved, where culture plays a role, and how to differentiate between the two in an age of Google searches, AI queries, and social media feeds. We will examine the challenges of living in today’s society and reflect on how great scholars, both women and men, confronted challenges in their time. Learn to spot red flags, ask better questions, and build a faith that outlasts the algorithm.

Sacred Framework: Intro to Maqasid Al-Shariah

Gain an introduction to the Maqasid al-Shari’ah—the higher objectives and wisdom behind Islamic law. This course explores the core purposes that guide Islamic teachings and helps students develop a deeper appreciation for how Islam seeks to preserve faith, life, intellect, family, and wealth in both personal and societal contexts.

Thursday - 8PM
SYLLABUS COMING SOON

Quranic Reading Workshop

This session is designed for individuals of all levels who seek to improve their Quran reading skills. Whether you are starting from the alphabet, working on fluency, or beginning memorization, this session will offer support on your level. Join us to develop confidence and accuracy while reading the Quran. Classes will be separate for sisters and brothers InshaAllah.

Our Instructors

Say Salam to your teachers, each ready to share their knowledge.

Ustadh Abdurrahman Wood

Instructor

Ustadh Abdurrahman Justin Wood is an American Imam, Speaker, and Leader born and raised in Columbus, Ohio. He accepted Islam at age 17 after studying the life of Malcolm X and researching religion and philosophy. To gain a deeper understanding of Arabic and Islamic sciences, he traveled to Makkah, Saudi Arabia and completed an American Imam certificate course. Upon returning to the United States, he focused on helping young American Muslims embrace the relevance and practicality of Islam, establishing and supporting youth initiatives in multiple communities. He also serves as Ameer of AlMaghrib's Columbus, Ohio.

Ustadh Mohamed Noor

Instructor

Ustadh Mohamed Noor is a dedicated instructor at Siraaj Institute and Ma'had ibn Taymiyah. Involved in Dawah since age 15, he has more than a decade of experience in Islamic education and outreach. For almost a decade, he has taught Arabic grammar, Hadith, Tafseer, Aqeedah, and Fiqh. Ustadh Mohamed began his studies in Columbus, Ohio, graduating from the Ma'had at Masjid ibn Taymiyyah, and has studied with scholars from Somalia, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Nigeria, and Senegal. He also pursued advanced studies abroad in Egypt and Saudi Arabia. His diverse educational background, along with his commitment to nurturing the next generation of Muslim scholars and leaders, make him a highly respected figure within the community.

Sheikh Hassan Noor

Instructor

Sheikh Hassan Noor is an accomplished scholar specializing in Islamic law. He graduated with First Class honors from the College of Islamic Law at Imam University in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and is currently pursuing a Master’s Degree in legal theory at Al-Madinah International University in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

With years of teaching experience at Ibnu Taymiyah Islamic Center in Columbus, Sheikh Hassan is known for his insightful presentations on Islamic jurisprudence. He has taught courses in Arabic, Fiqh, Legal Theory, and Islamic Texts.
